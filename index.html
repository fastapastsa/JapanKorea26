Japan · Days 1–5

Tokyo

28 September – 2 October · 4 nights at Mitsui Garden Hotel Ueno. Old-Tokyo lanes in the north-east, then the west side, then the bookshops and back-alleys. All three full days are walkable clusters — no criss-crossing the city.

South Korea · Days 5–8

Seoul

2 – 5 October · 3 nights at Sejong Hotel Myeongdong. Selena joins here. Two full days only, so both are tightly clustered: palaces and the old quarters on Saturday, the Seongsu side of the river on Sunday.

South Korea · Days 8–10

Busan

5 – 7 October · 2 nights at Hotel Foret Premier Nampo. One and a half days, and the film festival opens while we're there. The plan runs east along the coast in a single line so nothing doubles back.

Japan · Days 10–14

Fukuoka

7 – 11 October · 4 nights at Best Western Plus Tenjin-Minami. The only place on the trip none of us have been. Itoshima by car, a Dazaifu day, then Hakata, Ohori Park and an optional seaside afternoon.

Japan · Days 14–18

Kyoto & Osaka

11 – 15 October · 4 nights at Hotel Forza Kyoto Shijo Kawaramachi. Osaka, Higashiyama, Fushimi and Uji across four Kyoto-based nights.

Japan · Days 18–20

Tokyo, second stay

15 – 17 October · 2 nights at Henn na Hotel Akasaka. Akasaka down to Tokyo Tower on arrival day, one day still undecided, then home.
